INFORMATION ABOUT THE CAR RENTAL SORT ORDER, SPONSORED PLACEMENTS AND PERSONALISED PRICING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### How does the 'Recommended' sort order work?

The 'Recommended' sort order uses a variety of factors to rank vehicles in an order which ensures you see the best 'Value for Money' at the top of your search results. Commission received from car rental suppliers influences this order.

### What does 'Value for Money' mean?

The term 'Value for Money' describes the variables which influence a customer's likelihood to purchase car rental. The commission we (CarTrawler) receive from car rental suppliers recognises the role we (CarTrawler) play in the booking and influences how we determine the overall 'Value for Money'.

### What variables are considered in the sort order?

In identifying the best 'Value for Money' for our customers, our sort order considers a number of variables, including:

*   The booking date and location of the search.
    
*   The duration of the rental period.
    
*   Customer ratings of car rental suppliers based on CSAT scores and NPS.
    
*   The historic behaviour of customers in relation to past purchases and historic pricing of similar car rental results.
    
*   The commission we receive from car rental suppliers or insurance partners.
    

### How do Sponsored Placements work?

Some travel partners work with selected car rental suppliers. Sponsored placements may be featured at the top of the search results due to payments received by the travel partner from the selected car rental supplier. Customers can identify these sponsored placements where they are highlighted in a branded placement at the top of the search results.

We may personalise the pricing for services available through the booking engine on the basis of automated decision-making.
